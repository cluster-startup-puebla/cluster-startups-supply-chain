# Privacy Notice

**Last updated:** 11 August 2026

This notice is issued under Mexico's Federal Law on Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties (LFPDPPP), published in the Official Gazette of the Federation on 20 March 2025 and in force since 21 March 2025, and its Regulations.

This is a courtesy translation. In case of any discrepancy, the Spanish version of this notice prevails.

## 1. Identity and address of the data controller

**Cluster de Startups e Innovación A.C.** (the "Cluster"), with address at Av. Roble 16, Zona VW, Sanctorum, Puebla, Mexico, is responsible for the processing and protection of the personal data you provide.

For any matter related to this notice, write to us at **hola@alvarocastillo.dev**.

## 2. Personal data we process

When you fill in the contact form on this site, we collect directly from you:

- Full name
- Company or organization
- Role or job title *(optional)*
- The profile you select: industry or supplier, startup or founder, investor, university or academia, or other
- The challenge or interest you describe *(optional)*
- Email address
- Phone or WhatsApp number *(optional)*

**We do not collect sensitive personal data.** We neither request nor process information about racial or ethnic origin, health status, genetic or biometric information, religious, philosophical or moral beliefs, union membership, political opinions or sexual preference. We do not collect financial or asset data either.

Please do not include sensitive, financial or asset data in the open field where you describe your challenge.

## 3. Purposes of processing

### 3.1 Necessary purposes

These purposes are essential to handle your request and give rise to your relationship with the Cluster:

- Contacting you through the means you provided.
- Understanding the challenge or interest you raise and classifying it according to the profile you selected.
- Routing your case to the relevant area, company, startup or institution in the ecosystem.
- Following up on the conversation and keeping an internal record of requests received.

### 3.2 Additional purposes

These purposes are not necessary to assist you, and you may refuse them without affecting how your request is handled:

- Informing you about calls for proposals, events, programs and Cluster activities.
- Inviting you to matchmaking sessions with industry, startups or academic institutions.
- Producing aggregated, anonymous statistics about the challenges we receive.

If you do not want your data processed for these additional purposes, write to **hola@alvarocastillo.dev** with the subject "Additional purposes" at any time, including when you submit the form. Refusing is not a reason for us to stop handling your request.

## 4. Means to limit the use or disclosure of your data

You may limit the use or disclosure of your personal data by sending your request to the email address given in this notice. We will process your request and confirm it through the same channel.

## 5. ARCO rights

You have the right to know what personal data we hold about you, what we use it for and the conditions of that use (**Access**); to request correction of your information when it is outdated, inaccurate or incomplete (**Rectification**); to ask us to remove it from our records when you consider it is not being used in accordance with the principles, duties and obligations set out in the applicable regulations (**Cancellation**); and to object to the use of your personal data for specific purposes (**Opposition**).

### How to exercise them

Send your request to **hola@alvarocastillo.dev**. So that we can act on it, please include:

1. Your name and the channel through which you want to receive our reply.
2. Documents proving your identity or, where applicable, the legal representation of whoever files the request.
3. A clear and precise description of the personal data your request concerns.
4. Any other detail that helps us locate your data.

### Deadlines

We will inform you of our determination within a maximum of **twenty business days** from the date we receive your request. If it is granted, we will act on it within the **fifteen business days** following the date we communicate our reply. These deadlines may be extended once for an equal period where the circumstances justify it; we will notify you if that happens.

Exercising ARCO rights is free of charge. You would only need to cover justified shipping costs or the cost of reproduction in copies or other formats, where applicable.

## 6. Withdrawal of consent

You may withdraw the consent you gave us for the processing of your personal data at any time, through the same channel and with the same requirements set out for ARCO rights.

Please note that in some cases we may not be able to act on your request immediately due to legal obligations, or that withdrawal may mean we can no longer handle your original request. We will let you know if that is the case.

## 7. Transfers and remittances

**We do not sell, assign or trade your personal data with third parties.**

To operate this site and handle your request we rely on hosting and infrastructure providers acting as data processors, processing data solely on behalf of and under the instructions of the Cluster. These remittances do not require your consent under the applicable regulations. Some of these providers may store information on servers located outside Mexico.

When your challenge requires connecting you with a company, startup or institution in the ecosystem, we will tell you and **ask for your consent before sharing your data** with them.

## 8. Use of cookies

This site uses a strictly functional cookie named `NEXT_LOCALE`, which stores the language you selected so the site can be shown in that language on your next visits. It is not used for advertising or profiling.

You can delete or block cookies from your browser settings; if you do, the site will keep working and will simply stop remembering your language preference.

## 9. Changes to this notice

This notice may be amended due to changes in applicable regulations, in our internal practices or in the way we operate.

We will publish any changes on this same page and update the "Last updated" date shown at the top. We recommend reviewing it periodically. Where a change is substantial, we will notify you at the email address you provided.

## 10. Supervisory authority

If you believe your right to personal data protection has been infringed, you may turn to the **Secretaría Anticorrupción y Buen Gobierno**, the authority that assumed personal data protection functions after INAI was dissolved.
